About ConnectHub

ConnectHub is a social media website prototype. You can create posts, attach pictures or videos, like posts, add comments, save posts and try the chat interface.

This version runs in your browser. To make it a real social network where different people can register, communicate online and share posts across devices, you will need a secure backend and database.
